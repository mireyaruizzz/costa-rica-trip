Before you go

Logistics & packing

  • Rental car return: only the driver heading straight to the airport should return the car to Alamo — they'll get a shuttle. Everyone else needs an Uber. Going from the hotel instead? Book the hotel shuttle 24 hours ahead.
  • Insurance: mid-tier is on the reservation, but premium (~$150 more) is worth it for peace of mind.
  • Getting around at Dulce Olas: the motorcycle, the quad, or the surfboards left out for you.
  • Last day: on this itinerary you'll have until about noon to explore San José before heading to the airport.
  • Packing: carry-on only, no need for a lot of clothing.
  • Depart Costa Rica: August 25.

Check in with SANSA an hour before the flight — plenty of buffer either way. From LAX, park at one of the lots down the road; the ones running about $10/day include a shuttle. Alamo car rental is walking distance from the Jiménez airstrip — get the premium insurance (~$150 more than mid), then hit the BM grocery store in town before heading to the guesthouse.
