Before you go

Logistics & packing

  • Rental car return: only the driver heading straight to the airport should return the car to Alamo — they'll get a shuttle. Everyone else needs an Uber. Going from the hotel instead? Book the hotel shuttle 24 hours ahead.
  • Insurance: mid-tier is on the reservation, but premium (~$150 more) is worth it for peace of mind.
  • Getting around at Dulce Olas: the motorcycle, the quad, or the surfboards left out for you.
  • Last day: on this itinerary you'll have until about noon to explore San José before heading to the airport.
  • Packing: carry-on only, no need for a lot of clothing — Avianca's fare includes one free carry-on (up to 10kg/22lb) per segment, just no checked bag, so pack light and you're covered.
  • Wifi at both Airbnbs: login details and (for Manuel Antonio) the lockbox code only unlock 48 hours before check-in — don't worry if you can't find them earlier.
  • Message hosts ahead of arrival: WhatsApp Pura Natura at +506 8969 6680 for Manuel Antonio; for San José, coordinate check-in timing with Javier through the Airbnb app since it's building-staff check-in.
  • Emergency contact: James's mom — 951-375-7353. Keep this handy for yourself and share with your parents in case anything comes up.
  • Depart Costa Rica: August 25, SJO 15:10 via San Salvador, landing LAX ~22:10.
